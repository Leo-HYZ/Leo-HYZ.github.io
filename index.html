01 / ABOUT

Research with
real-world relevance.

I am a doctoral researcher in energy economics at Beijing Institute of Technology. My work combines economic modeling, optimization, and data-driven analysis to understand climate mitigation and low-carbon development.

I am particularly interested in the interaction between policy design, population change, power systems, and emerging electricity demand from data centers.
